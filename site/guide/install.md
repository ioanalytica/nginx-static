<!--@include: ../../chart/README.md#install-->

## Pin a version

For production, name one of the
[released versions](https://github.com/ioanalytica/nginx-static/releases)
with `--version`. [Versioning](/reference/versioning) explains the scheme,
the [Changelog](/reference/changelog) lists the versions, and
[Verify a release](/security/verify) checks a chart's signature before it is
installed.
